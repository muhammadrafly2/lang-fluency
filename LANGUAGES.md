# Multi-Language Support Guide

Nexora supports four languages with specialized coaching for each. This guide explains the unique features and cultural considerations for each language.

## 🇺🇸 English

### Speech Recognition
- Language Code: `en-US`
- Browser Support: Excellent (Chrome, Edge, Safari)

### AI Coaching Focus
- Natural expressions and idioms
- Cultural context from English-speaking countries
- Formal vs. informal register
- Business English and professional communication

### Example Practice Topics
- Job interviews and networking
- Academic discussions
- Casual conversations and small talk
- Technical presentations

---

## 🇯🇵 Japanese (日本語)

### Speech Recognition
- Language Code: `ja-JP`
- Browser Support: Good (Chrome, Edge recommended)

### AI Coaching Focus
- **Keigo (敬語)** - Honorific language levels
  - Sonkeigo (尊敬語) - Respectful language
  - Kenjōgo (謙譲語) - Humble language
  - Teineigo (丁寧語) - Polite language
- **Writing Systems**
  - Kanji usage and readings
  - Hiragana and Katakana
  - Proper particle usage (は、が、を、に、で、etc.)
- **Cultural Nuances**
  - Appropriate greetings for different situations
  - Business etiquette (名刺交換, etc.)
  - Seasonal expressions (季節の挨拶)

### Example Practice Topics
- Business meetings (会議)
- Restaurant ordering (レストラン)
- Shopping and customer service (買い物)
- Formal introductions (自己紹介)

### Common Corrections
- Particle mistakes
- Verb conjugation (especially potential, passive, causative)
- Keigo level appropriateness
- Word order in complex sentences

## 🇰🇷 Korean (한국어)

### Speech Recognition
- Language Code: `ko-KR`
- Browser Support: Good (Chrome, Edge recommended)

### AI Coaching Focus
- **Honorifics System**
  - 존댓말 (Jondaetmal) - Formal/polite speech
  - 반말 (Banmal) - Informal speech
  - 높임말 (Nopimmal) - Honorific expressions
- **Hangul Pronunciation**
  - Consonant and vowel combinations
  - Final consonants (받침)
  - Sound changes (연음, 경음화, etc.)
- **Cultural Context**
  - Age-based hierarchy
  - Workplace formality
  - Family relationships and titles

### Example Practice Topics
- Meeting elders (어르신과 대화)
- Workplace communication (직장 생활)
- Ordering food (음식 주문)
- Making friends (친구 사귀기)

### Common Corrections
- Honorific verb endings (-습니다, -세요, etc.)
- Subject/object markers (이/가, 을/를)
- Informal vs. formal register mixing
- Pronunciation of double consonants

- Language Code: `es-ES` (European Spanish)
- Browser Support: Excellent (Chrome, Edge, Safari)

### AI Coaching Focus
- **Formal vs. Informal**

  - Vosotros vs. Ustedes (regional differences)

  - Present, preterite, imperfect
  - Subjunctive mood
  - Reflexive verbs
- **Regional Variations**
  - European vs. Latin American Spanish
  - Vocabulary differences
  - Pronunciation variations
- **Cultural Context**
  - Greetings and social customs
  - Business etiquette
  - Meal times and social gatherings

### Example Practice Topics
- Travel and tourism (viajes)
- Business meetings (reuniones de negocios)
- Social gatherings (fiestas y eventos)

- Por vs. Para
- Subjunctive mood triggers
- Gender agreement (masculine/feminine)
- Accent marks and pronunciation

## 🎯 Tips for All Languages

### Maximizing Your Practice

1. **Start with Your Level**
   - Beginner: Focus on basic vocabulary and simple sentences
   - Intermediate: Practice complex grammar and natural expressions
   - Advanced: Work on nuance, idioms, and cultural fluency

2. **Use Voice Input**
   - Helps with pronunciation
   - Builds speaking confidence
   - More natural conversation flow

3. **Review Feedback**
   - Read all corrections carefully
   - Try to use corrected forms in next messages
   - Ask for clarification if needed

4. **Practice Regularly**
   - Daily 15-minute sessions are better than weekly long sessions
   - Build your streak for motivation
   - Try different practice modes

5. **Cultural Context Matters**
   - Learn when to use formal vs. informal speech
   - Understand cultural references
   - Practice appropriate responses for situations

### Switching Languages

To change your target language:
1. Open **Settings** (gear icon)
2. Select **Target Language** dropdown
3. Choose your desired language
4. Click **Apply Settings**

The speech recognition will automatically update to match your selected language.

## 🔧 Technical Notes

### Speech Recognition Accuracy

- **Best**: Chrome and Edge browsers
- **Good**: Safari (may have limitations)
- **Limited**: Firefox (speech recognition support varies)

### Voice Selection

Different languages have different TTS voices available:
- The voice list in Settings shows all available voices for your browser
- Look for voices matching your target language
- Some browsers have more voices than others

### API Considerations

- The AI model (Nvidia Nemotron) supports all four languages
- Response quality is excellent for all supported languages
- Feedback is provided in English for clarity, but can be adjusted
